# Third-party projects and distribution scope

v0.1.0 includes no third-party source tree, engine executable, network weight,
or game archive. Its runtime uses only Python's standard library;
setuptools is a build dependency, not vendored code.

The paper's broader experimental setting involves projects including:

- [KataGo](https://github.com/lightvector/KataGo)
- [Go Attack](https://github.com/AlignmentResearch/go_attack)
- [Leela Zero](https://github.com/leela-zero/leela-zero)
- [ELF / ELF OpenGo](https://github.com/pytorch/ELF)

These links identify external projects, not dependencies required by this
release, endorsements, or permission to redistribute their assets. Their
source, binaries, datasets, and weights retain their respective terms.

The repository's Apache-2.0 license applies to its original software,
documentation, and synthetic examples. The Springer paper, typesetting
templates, and signed publication agreement are not included or relicensed.
